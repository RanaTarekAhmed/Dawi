💊 Dawi
🤝 Connecting medicine donations with the people who need them
Dawi is a community-focused platform that brings donors, beneficiaries, and pharmacists together to make better use of available medicines, reduce waste, and make access to needed products easier.
✨ Why Dawi?
- ♻️ Less medicine waste — gives suitable surplus medicines a chance to reach someone who needs them.
- ❤️ Community support — creates a connection between people willing to donate and people looking for support.
- 🧑‍⚕️ Pharmacist involvement — pharmacists review donated products before they become available.
- 🏥 Trusted pharmacy network — helps people discover verified pharmacies and the products associated with them.
- 🔎 Clear product information — presents details such as category, quantity, expiry date, and availability.
- 📦 Organized requests — lets beneficiaries request available quantities and follow the progress of their requests.
- 📅 Coordinated collection — supports arranging collection dates and recording completed collections.
- 🔔 Helpful updates — notifications keep users informed about important request and collection changes.
- 🛡️ Safer access — role-based permissions help ensure that users, pharmacists, and administrators access the features intended for them.
🌱 The Impact
Dawi aims to turn unused, suitable medicines into meaningful support.

By combining community donations with pharmacist review and an organized request process, the platform helps:
- Reduce avoidable pharmaceutical waste.
- Make donated products easier to discover.
- Support a more organized donation and collection journey.
- Encourage collaboration between donors, beneficiaries, and pharmacies.
